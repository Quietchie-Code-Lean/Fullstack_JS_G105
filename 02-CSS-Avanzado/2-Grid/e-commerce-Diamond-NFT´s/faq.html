Frequently Asked Questions

What is an NFT?

An NFT (Non-Fungible Token) is a unique digital asset stored on a blockchain, representing ownership of digital art, music, or collectibles.

How can I buy an NFT?

You can purchase NFTs using cryptocurrencies such as Ethereum (ETH), USDT, or Bitcoin (BTC). Just connect your digital wallet and complete the transaction.

Are all artworks unique?

Yes. Every NFT in our gallery is one-of-a-kind, verified through blockchain technology.

Can I resell my NFT?

Absolutely! You can trade your NFTs in any compatible marketplace at your desired price.

What payment methods do you accept?

We mainly accept crypto payments: ETH, USDT, BTC, and MATIC. Soon we’ll add fiat payment options.
